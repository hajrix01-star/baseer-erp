## Business conclusion

In Noorix, purchases and operational expenses are financial outflow invoices, not merely uploaded receipts. A saved active invoice combines the source receipt data, tax calculation, selected supplier/category, one or more paying vaults, balanced ledger entries, payment-allocation rows, and audit evidence.

The reviewed outflow kinds include purchase, expense, HR expense, fixed expense, salary, and employee advance. Baseer will treat purchases and ordinary/fixed expenses as the Finance scope; payroll and advances remain separate future modules even when Noorix uses the same invoice storage path.

## Inputs and validation observed in Noorix

A supplier invoice can hold:

- an internal serial generated by the system;
- the supplier's own invoice number;
- supplier, category, expense line, employee where relevant;
- business/transaction date and original invoice date;
- gross total, net amount, VAT amount, and taxability;
- a single paying vault or several vault allocations;
- notes and a receipt/image/PDF attachment;
- a fixed-expense coverage period or warranty-follow-up details where applicable; and
- idempotency key for safe retry.

For a taxable purchase/expense with a supplier, Noorix requires a supplier invoice number. It creates a normalized duplicate key per company + supplier + active supplier-invoice number. The normalization removes spaces, unifies Arabic/Persian/Latin digits, and ignores letter case. Thus visually different forms of the same supplier number cannot be entered twice.

The paying-vault allocations must be positive and add exactly to the gross invoice total. Every selected vault must be active, unarchived, company-owned, and allowed as a payment method. If multiple allocations are used, the invoice header does not pretend that one vault is the source of payment; the allocation rows are the evidence.

## Accounting effect observed in Noorix

For each vault allocation, Noorix posts a balanced outflow entry:

- debit the approved expense/purchase account or an explicitly mapped category account; and
- credit the linked account of the paying vault.

The system saves a durable invoice-vault allocation for each credit leg and records an audit entry. Batch creation is atomic: if one invoice fails, the batch transaction rolls back. A request/operation idempotency key prevents accidental duplicate posting.

## Suppliers and attachments observed in Noorix

Suppliers are company-scoped. A supplier tax number is unique among active suppliers of that company. Noorix uses soft deletion and prevents deletion while active invoices or active expense lines still reference the supplier.

The reviewed invoice attachment flow accepts image formats and PDF, attaches one current file path/name to the invoice, and writes an audit event when the attachment is replaced or removed.

## Critical Baseer decisions and improvements

### 1. Direct editing in an open fiscal period

Noorix supports direct editing of an active outflow invoice and rebuilding its current ledger rows and payment allocations. This is suitable for Baseer's private, day-to-day workflow.

Baseer adopts this behavior only while the invoice business date belongs to an open fiscal period. The server validates the company and permissions, recalculates the current gross/net/VAT result, rebuilds the current active ledger and payment allocations atomically, preserves document number/source identity, and records a complete before/after audit event. After the period is closed or locked, direct edit is denied and the correction path follows `../foundation/OPEN_PERIOD_FINANCIAL_EDITING_GATE_A_DECISION.md`.

### 2. Tax evidence is snapshotted

Baseer will retain the effective VAT rate, treatment, net, VAT, gross, rounding rule, and VAT account for each saved invoice version. An authorized edit in an open period recalculates the current version and records the prior values in audit history; changing company VAT settings alone cannot rewrite existing documents.

### 3. Source receipt identity is controlled

For a supplier invoice, Baseer requires a supplier source-invoice number whenever it exists. The duplicate check is company + supplier + normalized source number and applies while the original document is active. If no supplier invoice exists, the user must select a documented reason rather than entering a made-up number.

### 4. Attachments are evidence, not replaceable history

Baseer will use the approved file-metadata platform. A receipt is stored as versioned evidence with checksum, uploader, time, retention state, and access control. A later replacement creates a new version and preserves the historical evidence; it does not silently delete the original file after posting.

### 5. Supplier history is preserved

After a supplier has any posted or imported historical document, Baseer archives rather than deletes it. Name, tax number, category, and source mappings used in historical records are snapshots/evidence, not mutable reinterpretations of old invoices.

### 6. Period, company, and authorization controls apply everywhere

Create, direct edit in an open period, cancellation, reversal, allocation changes, attachment evidence actions, and import all require the authorized company context and the fiscal-period policy recorded in the accounting-foundation study.

## Migration requirements

For every Noorix outflow invoice, Baseer must retain or map: source invoice ID, internal number, supplier source number, kind, supplier, category/expense line, transaction date, original invoice date, net/VAT/gross, VAT treatment, paying-vault allocations, status, attachment evidence metadata, creator, and source checksum.

An ambiguous supplier match, duplicate normalized supplier number, unmatched category/account, unmapped vault, missing source amount, or missing attachment file is an import exception. It cannot be silently converted to a generic expense.


### Category mapping authority: utilities and source-code safety

`apps/api/src/finance/noorix-category-mapping.ts` is the canonical resolver for the Noorix baseline **leaf** category codes. The importer must retain the source category ID and code on its receipt, then resolve the target category again inside the target company. It may post only to an active `isPosting=true` category owned by that company.

For the small-company Baseer default, the category **`UTIL-001` — مرافق وخدمات** is one posting category. Noorix leaves `E3-2` (electricity), `E3-3` (telecommunications), and `E3-4` (water), and `E3-5` (gas) map semantically to it. This is deliberately a semantic map, not a code match. Noorix `EXP-009` is an employee-benefits group, not Baseer's former electricity code; an import must never infer a match merely because two systems reuse a code.

`E9-3` (GOSI) maps to `E4-2`. Noorix `E9-1` (travel tickets), `E9-2` (medical insurance), any parent category, custom source category, or inactive target category becomes a review exception. The owner must select an approved posting category before that document can be imported. No exception falls back to a generic expense.

Existing Baseer companies are not rewritten: old separate utility records remain historical data. After the owner runs the audited finance-seed refresh to version 5, `UTIL-001` is available for new work. Old categories may be archived only through their normal lifecycle after their history is reconciled.
## Gate B test requirements

- company and user isolation for suppliers, invoices, attachments, accounts, and vault allocations;
- exact equality of allocation total and invoice gross total after currency rounding;
- a selected category maps to a valid company account and type;
- supplier source-number duplicate prevention, including Arabic/Persian digit and whitespace variants;
- tax snapshots remain unchanged when company default VAT changes;
- idempotent create and atomic batch rollback;
- authorized open-period edit atomically rebuilds the current ledger/allocation effect and records the before/after audit evidence;
- closed/locked-period reversal or adjustment preserves the original document and makes the net financial effect auditable;
- file evidence access is authorized and version history/checksum remains available; and
- migration reconciles invoice count, gross/net/VAT totals, payment allocations, suppliers, attachments, and debit=credit totals per company.

## Owner decision needed before Finance implementation

The recommended personal-ERP default is: draft is optional. An authorized user may save and directly correct a purchase/expense invoice while its date is in an open fiscal period. The system still validates required tax/source information and records a reason such as cash receipt, receipt unavailable, or legacy import when a supplier invoice number does not exist.
